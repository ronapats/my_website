## Rule #1: Origin of Great Success
- Mindset is the key difference. **Burning Desire — you have to really want it.**
- Read your goals every morning and before bed; repetition trains your subconscious mind.

## Rule #2: Extreme Ownership
- **Event + Response = Outcome.** Be proactive.
- Bad + learning = growth. Bad + suffering = getting worse.
- Do more than others; don't blame others. **Your own life is your own choice.**

## Rule #3: Power of Perseverance
- Never let go. The ability to focus on long-term goals and keep going despite obstacles.
- **Challenges are an opportunity to grow and get stronger — embrace them.**
- To improve perseverance: have a dream/burning desire and write it down; build a growth mindset; be in a hardworking environment; focus and be mindful.

## Rule #4: Flow
- **Focus** (per Warren Buffett and Bill Gates).
- To improve focus: have a clear goal, prioritize your to-do list, stop multi-tasking.

## Rule #5: Super Self Discipline
- You have limited time — don't waste it.
- A keystone action (e.g. morning exercise) creates a domino effect on other actions — better focus, sleep, diet.
- Start small and simple. The 5-second rule: count 5...4...3...2...1 and act within five seconds.

## Rule #6: Unstoppable Self Confidence
- "Believe you can, and you're halfway there." Be → Do → Have.
- You are you — don't compare yourself with others. Your idol should be yourself, five years from now.
- Exercise builds positive energy and confidence. Fake it to build high power. Confidence brings success.

## Rule #7: Entrepreneurial Mindset
- Leadership needs EQ — succeeding in business is all about making connections.
- Big-picture, long-term thinking. Lifelong learning — ask questions, keep reading. Have great mentors.

**Be the best version of yourself!**
