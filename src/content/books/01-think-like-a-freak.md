Read on 14-15 Dec 2022.

It's okay to say "I don't know."

**Takeru Kobayashi — Hot-dog eating champion.** Experiment and get feedback:
- Adopt the tactics that work and get rid of those that don't
- Redefine the problem (Others: "How do I eat more hot dogs?" / Kobayashi: "How do I make hot dogs easier to eat?")
- Ignore the upper limit / artificial barriers

**Think like a child**
- Be curious and unbiased
- Not afraid to share ideas

**People respond to incentives** — design the right incentive scheme.
- Don't listen to what people say, watch what people do and really care about
- Think less about the ideal behavior of imaginary people and more about the actual behavior of real people
- Pay attention to how people respond, learn from it

King Solomon (two wives, one dead baby and one living baby — slice the baby).

Persuade people using stories.

**The upside of quitting**
- Sunk cost fallacy — throw good money after bad
- Premortem = find out what might go wrong before it's too late; gather everyone connected with a project and have them imagine that it launched and failed miserably
- Postmortem = learn what went wrong on a failed project
