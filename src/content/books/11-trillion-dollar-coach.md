## Ch.1 The caddie and the CEO
- To be a great manager, you have to be a great coach — the higher you climb, the more your success depends on making others successful.

## Ch.2 Your title makes you a manager, your people make you a leader
- Bring people around by making them feel valued — listen, pay attention.
- **Your title makes you a manager. Your people make you a leader.**
- **People are the foundation of any company's success.**
- **The primary job of each manager is to help people be more effective and to grow.**
  - Support = give people the tools, information, training, and coaching they need.
  - Respect = understand people's unique career goals and be sensitive to their life choices.
  - Trust = free people to do their jobs and make decisions.
- Start team meetings with personal, non-business topics to build rapport.
- "Get the 1:1 right" and "get the staff meeting right" are top management principles — prepare for one-on-ones; they're the best way to help people grow.
- Failure to decide can be as damaging as a wrong decision — do something, even if it's wrong.
- **Having a well-run process to reach a decision is as important as the decision itself — it gives the team confidence and keeps everyone moving.**
- **Lead based on first principles — you can argue opinions, but generally not principles everyone's already agreed on.**
- If you have the right product for the right market at the right time, go as fast as you can.
- Tell engineers the problem and the customer context, not the features — let them figure out the features; they'll provide a better solution.
- It's the CEO's job to manage the board, not the other way around.

## Ch.3 Build an envelope of trust
- Traits of coachability: honesty, humility, willingness to persevere and work hard, and constant openness to learning.
- Listen to people with full, undivided attention — it makes them feel valued.
- **A manager's job is to push the team to be more courageous — people fear taking risks for fear of failure.**
- **People are most effective when they can be themselves completely and bring their full identity to work.**

## Ch.4 Team first
- **Work the team, then the problem** — first ensure the right team is in place.
- Understand people's individual limitations and see how they can mesh with the rest of the team.
- Pick the right players: ability to learn fast, willingness to work hard, integrity, grit, empathy, team-first attitude.
- **Solve the biggest problem — tackle it first.**
- Winning right — strive to win with commitment, teamwork, and integrity.
- Fill the gaps between people: listen, observe, and fill communication/understanding gaps.
- **Get the team right and you'll get the issue right.**

## Ch.5 The power of love
- To care about people, ask about their lives outside work, understand their families, and show up when things get rough.
- **Always build communities, inside and outside of work — a place is stronger when people are connected.**
- What matters most are the bonds forged by caring for each other and the common good.
