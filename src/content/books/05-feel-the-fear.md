## 1. What are you afraid of… and why?
- All you have to do to diminish your fear is develop more trust in your ability to handle whatever comes your way.
- Lack of trust in yourself is stopping you from getting what you want out of life.
- Develop trust until you can say: **"Whatever happens to me, given any situation, I can handle it."**

## 2. Can't you make it go away?
- Truth 1: the fear will never go away as long as you continue to grow.
- **Truth 2: the only way to get rid of the fear of doing something is to go out and do it.** Do it often enough and you'll no longer be afraid.
- Truth 3: the only way to feel better about myself is to go out and do it — you'll feel stronger once you have.
- Truth 4: everyone feels fear on unfamiliar territory, not just you.
- Truth 5: pushing through fear is less frightening than living with the underlying fear that comes from helplessness.
- **The answer to "what if" is "I'll handle it."**

## 3. From pain to power
- The secret to handling fear is moving from a position of pain to a position of power.
- Power within the self: power over your perceptions, over how you react, to act, and to love.
- Each time you're in better control of your life, you move toward power, reducing fear.
- Pain-to-power vocabulary — reframe "I can't" into what you'd gain instead; "it's not my fault" into full responsibility; "if only" into a lesson for next time.
- **We make decisions based on the confines of our comfortable space. Expand your comfort zone. Take a risk — you will feel great once you've done it.**
- With each risk, you become more powerful; your comfort zone expands too.

## 4. Whether you want it or not… it's yours
- **The truth is you really are in control** — you are consciously or unconsciously choosing your current state. If you can create your own misery, you can create your own joy.
- **You are the cause of your reactions to everything that happens to you.**
- Seven definitions of taking responsibility: never blame anyone else; never blame yourself; be aware of when you're not taking responsibility; handle the negative "chatterbox" voice; be aware of payoffs that keep you stuck; figure out what you want and act on it; be aware of the multitude of choices in any situation.
- **Lead yourself instead of being led.**

## 5. Pollyanna rides again — positive attitudes
- Over 90% of what we worry about never happens.
- Positive words make us physically strong; negative words make us physically weak. Stop feeding yourself negative thoughts.
- Affirmations: "I relax, knowing I can handle it all." "I am powerful and I am loving and I have nothing to fear." Write them in present tense, positive, in your own words, specific.
- **Positive thinking changes everything in your life** and needs daily practice.
- **The key is to know you can lead a productive, meaningful life no matter the external circumstances.**

## 6. When "they" don't want you to grow
- Have a support group of friends who are consciously growing.
- Choose growth over unhealthy relationships.
- Win-win response to negative feedback: acknowledge their concern, assert your own faith in yourself, and ask for their faith too.
- **The most important thing is to be your own best friend. Whichever path makes you grow is the path to take.**

## 7. How to make a no-lose decision
- **There is really nothing to lose, only something to gain, whatever choices you make.**
- Security is not having things, it's handling things. Answer every "what if" with "I can handle it" and the fear disappears.
- You are not a failure if you don't make it; you're a success because you tried.
- **If I'm not making any mistakes, I am not learning and growing.**
- **The trick in life is not to worry about making a wrong decision; it's learning when to correct it.**

## 8. How whole is your "whole life"?
- **Commitment = consciously giving 100% even if something is temporary.** While you're there, commit fully.
- Magic Duo = 100% Commitment + "I count" — helps you focus and achieve fulfillment.

## 9. Just nod your head — say "yes!"
- **We can't control the world, but we can control our reactions to it.**
- Saying yes to the universe reduces fear and creates meaning.
- **Adopt the attitude: "It's all happening perfectly. Let's see what good I can create from this situation."**
- Ask: what can I learn? How can I better myself as a result?

## 10. Choosing love and trust
- **You must become what you want to attract.**
- Two types of volunteers: those who know they count, and those proving they're "good people."

## 11. Filling the inner void
- Higher self = a source of positive thoughts and energy, moving above hate, fear, and negativity.
- **Your subconscious mind believes what your conscious mind tells it, whether or not it's true.**
- Say to yourself when facing a desired outcome: "It is all happening perfectly. There is nothing to fear." — you've let go of the outcome.
- **Visualization: imagine yourself approaching the goal with power and confidence, "as if" you had no fear.**

## 12. There is plenty of time
- Take action — nothing works unless you do the work.
- **Everyday of your life, come from knowing that you count. Everything you do has an effect.**
- **If you knew you could handle anything that came your way, what would you have to fear? Nothing.**
- **Whatever happens, I'll handle it!**
