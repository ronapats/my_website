## Product
- Viability — will we earn a profit? Desirability — do people want it? Feasibility — can we build it?
- What is the job to be done? Is it unique? Is there a large addressable need? What will get people talking? Emotional + Functional.
- Adoption scale: easy to purchase (channel), low pricing, ease of use, superior benefits (USP).

## Marketing
- Segment, Target, Position — know who your customer is, and who is NOT.
- Manage your brand by managing your touchpoints (web, ads, email, customer service) to resonate with customers and differentiate from competitors.
- Sustained competitive advantage — VRIO framework: is your product Valuable, Rare, costly to Imitate, Organized?

## Decision process
- Stop & think → gather facts → brainstorm solutions → decide.
- Problem solving basics: the key is **structuring the problem** (problem definition); the goal is to solve it with the highest benefit and lowest cost.
  - Understand the situation
  - Identify root cause — keep asking why and how
  - Develop action plan
  - Execute
- Divergence (have lots of ideas) → Convergence (throw out bad ideas).
