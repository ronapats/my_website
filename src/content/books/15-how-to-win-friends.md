## Part One: Fundamental techniques in handling people
- Instead of condemning people, try to understand them — figure out why they do what they do.
- Give honest and sincere appreciation.
- The only way to influence people is to talk about what they want and show them how to get it.
- "The ability to get the other person's point of view and see things from their angle, as well as your own" is the secret to success.
- Arouse in the other person an eager want — "he who can do this has the whole world with him."

## Part Two: Ways to make people like you
- You make more friends in two months by becoming genuinely interested in others than in two years trying to get others interested in you.
- Greet people with animation and enthusiasm.
- A smile says "I like you, you make me happy, I am glad to see you" — like dogs, we're glad to see those who are glad to see us.
- A person's name is the sweetest and most important sound in any language.
- Give exclusive attention to the person speaking to you — nothing is so flattering.
- Be a good listener. Encourage others to talk about themselves — people are a hundred times more interested in themselves than in you.
- Talk in terms of the other person's interests; make them feel important, and do it sincerely.

## Part Three: How to win people to your way of thinking
- If you're going to prove anything, don't let anybody know it — do it subtly.
- "You cannot teach a man anything; you can only help him find it within himself." (Galileo)
- Admitting you may be wrong stops all argument and inspires the other person to be just as open-minded.
- Telling someone straight out they're wrong only strips them of dignity and makes you unwelcome.
- Show respect for the other person's opinions. Never say "you're wrong."
