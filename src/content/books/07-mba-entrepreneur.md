- "If you want something you've never had, you must be willing to do something you've never done."
- You don't have to have a perfectly planned idea — what matters is taking action and starting to make it a reality.
- When learning any piece of information, stop often and ask: "What is the point? Is it just nice to know, or is it essential?"
- Good fit is essential if you want to truly excel in a new role.
- In a resource-constrained startup, it's easier to focus on building a single solution first.
