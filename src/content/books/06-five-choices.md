## Introduction: feeling buried?
- If we're not careful, we go on mental autopilot, moving between stimulating inputs and missing what's uniquely meaningful.
- **Extraordinary Productivity = High Value Decision + Focused Attention + High Energy**

## Decision management

### Choice 1: Act on the important, don't react to the urgent
- Reactive brain vs. Thinking brain — true productivity requires the habit of being conscious and intentional about everything you do.
- Are there important things you routinely put off? Is it a conscious choice or unconscious avoidance?
- Are you comfortable saying no when appropriate? **What price am I paying, in terms of more important things, by spending my time here?**
- Essential skills for extraordinary productivity: **Pause** your reactive brain, **Clarify** what's coming at you, **Decide** whether it's worth your time and energy.

### Choice 2: Go for extraordinary, don't settle for ordinary
- To harness your brain power, it needs direction and vision — a blueprint.
- **What are the most important roles in your life today?** Identify the few most important roles, then define what success looks like in each.
- **The goal is a harmonious relationship among your roles that gives satisfaction, both in the moment and over time.**

## Attention management

### Choice 3: Schedule the big rocks, don't sort gravel
- Decide what's most important, then schedule your weeks so your best attention and energy go there first.
- Choose Big Rocks (important priorities) first, Gravel (less important) later — schedule Big Rocks into the calendar.
- **The key to true productivity is not to get more things done, but to get the right things done.**

### Choice 4: Rule your technology, don't let it rule you
- Set rules for email to eliminate wasted time.
- **Know which battles you can ignore, then set up automatic systems to keep them from distracting you from higher purposes.**
- Paper systems keep everything in one place; electronic systems risk everything being in every place.

## Energy management

### Choice 5: Fuel your fire, don't burn out
- To be extraordinarily productive, create a way of life that's intentional, consciously making high-value decisions almost every waking minute.
- **Mental energy comes from a Clear Powerful Purpose and a Healthy Physical Body.**
- Five energy drivers:
  - **Move** — what helps your body helps your brain
  - **Eat** — good quality food optimizes performance
  - **Sleep** — improves memory, vital to productivity
  - **Relax** — regular breaks improve productivity and creativity; skipping them leads to stress and exhaustion
  - **Connect** — positive connections; the human moment = physical presence + attention
